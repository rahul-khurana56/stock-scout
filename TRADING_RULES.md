# 🏛️ MY PROFESSIONAL TRADING RULES

> *"Amateurs focus on what they can make. Professionals focus obsessively on what they can lose. Protect capital first — profits take care of themselves."*

---

## ⚡ The 10 Commandments of Execution

1. **SETUP FIRST — NO SETUP = NO TRADE.**
   * If the chart doesn't show a clear, predefined signal/confluence, you do not touch the order button. Sitting on hands is a position.

2. **ENTRY + SL + QTY + MAX LOSS DECIDED BEFORE ENTRY.**
   * You must know your exact exit point and rupee risk before clicking Buy. Never enter and then look for where to place your stop.

3. **SCALP: MAX ₹3–4K RISK | NORMAL: MAX ₹5–6K RISK.**
   * Sizing is calculated strictly from risk:
     $$\text{Position Size} = \frac{\text{Defined Rupee Risk}}{\text{Stop Loss Distance (Points)}}$$
   * **Scalp Trade:** ₹3,000 – ₹4,000 max risk (e.g. 1,000 qty $\times$ 3.5 pt SL, or 600 qty $\times$ 5 pt SL).
   * **Normal / Grade-A Trade:** ₹5,000 – ₹6,000 max risk (e.g. 300 qty $\times$ 18 pt SL, or 500 qty $\times$ 10 pt SL).

4. **QUANTITY IS LOCKED AFTER ENTRY.**
   * Once your order is executed, your position size is fixed. No impulse additions, no mid-trade size doubling.

5. **NEVER ADD TO A LOSING POSITION.**
   * Averaging down on a red trade is 100% prohibited. Not "just a little," not "to lower the cost." Zero exceptions.

6. **SL HIT = TRADE OVER. RE-ENTRY = COMPLETELY NEW TRADE.**
   * When your stop loss is hit, accept the loss immediately. If you want to re-enter because the setup resets, it must be treated as a brand new trade with a fresh entry and fresh SL.

7. **NO REVENGE. NO RECOVERY TRADES.**
   * Never trade to "win back" lost money. Never switch to commodities (MCX/NatGas/Crude) in the evening to recover daytime equity losses.

8. **ONE POSITION AT A TIME.**
   * 100% focus on one clean execution. No juggling multiple option legs, indices, or instruments simultaneously.

9. **₹6K DAILY LOSS OR 2 CONSECUTIVE LOSSES = DONE.**
   * If your realized/unrealized loss reaches **-₹6,000**, or if you suffer **2 losses in a row**, you are finished for the day. Close the terminal and walk away.

10. **PROTECT CAPITAL. FOLLOW THE SYSTEM. LET TOMORROW HANDLE TOMORROW.**
    * Today's market is done. Tomorrow will bring fresh opportunities. Your only duty is preserving your capital so you are alive to trade them.

## 📋 Pre-Flight & Execution Checklist

### Phase 1: Pre-Entry Gatekeeper (Ask Before Every Click)
- [ ] **Setup Verified (Rule 1):** Is there a confirmed chart trigger / signal? Or am I feeling FOMO/boredom?
- [ ] **Risk Calculated (Rule 2 & 3):**
  - Scalp Trade $\rightarrow$ Max Risk = ₹3,000 – ₹4,000.
  - Normal Trade $\rightarrow$ Max Risk = ₹5,000 – ₹6,000.
- [ ] **SL in System Order (Rule 2):** Is the Stop Loss attached directly to the buy ticket?
- [ ] **Depth & Spread Checked:** Is the bid-ask spread tight with real volume?
- [ ] **Single Focus (Rule 8):** Do I have 0 other open positions right now?

### Phase 2: Active Trade Protocol (While In Trade)
- [ ] **Size Locked (Rule 4):** Quantity is final. Hands off the add button.
- [ ] **Zero Red Adds (Rule 5):** If price drops below entry, I will NOT add.
- [ ] **Calm Monitoring:** SL is in the system. Stop staring at 1-second candles. Check only at key intervals.
- [ ] **Trailing Winners:** Once in profit (+5 to +10 pts), trail SL into green to bank profit.

### Phase 3: Invalidation & Exit Protocol
- [ ] **Hit SL Cleanly (Rule 6):** Let the system take the loss. No moving SL further away.
- [ ] **Reset Mindset:** If the setup resets and triggers again, calculate fresh size and fresh SL as a new trade.
- [ ] **Mandatory 15-Min Cool-Down:** After any loss, step away from the keyboard for 15 minutes.

### Phase 4: Daily Session & Circuit Breakers
- [ ] **Daily Loss Tracked:** Is net P&L within the -₹6,000 limit?
- [ ] **Strike 2 Rule (Rule 9):** Have I taken 2 consecutive losses? $\rightarrow$ If yes, **CLOSE APP IMMEDIATELY**.
- [ ] **No Evening Recovery (Rule 7):** Do not touch MCX/Natural Gas/Crude to recover daytime losses.
- [ ] **Protect Capital (Rule 10):** Log the trades into the journal and shut down the trading terminal.
